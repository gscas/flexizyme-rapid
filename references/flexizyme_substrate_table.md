## NCAAs by Functional Category for RaPID Libraries

### Cyclization Warheads
- **ClAc-D-Phe/D-Ala/Gly** — Thioether cyclization (most common)
- **BrAc-D-Phe** — Faster thioether cyclization
- **Acr-D-Phe** — Michael addition cyclization (reversible)

### N-Methyl Amino Acids (improve membrane permeability)
- N-Me-Phe, N-Me-Ala, N-Me-Leu, N-Me-Val, N-Me-Ile, N-Me-Ser, N-Me-Thr
- Typically incorporated via dFx (N-Me-AA-CME ester)
- 1–3 N-Me residues per 10-mer library typical

### D-Amino Acids (improve proteolytic stability)
- D-Phe, D-Ala, D-Ser, D-Val, D-Leu, D-Pro
- Lower charging yield than L-enantiomers
- Position at exposed, non-contact residues post-hit

### β-Amino Acids (extend chemical space)
- β-Phe, β-Ala, β-HomoAla, β-HomoPhe
- Via dFx (aromatic β-AA) or aFx (aliphatic)

### Photo-Crosslinkers
- Bpa (p-benzoylphenylalanine) — UV 365nm crosslink
- Tmd-Phe (4,4′-dimethoxytrityl-Phe) — 365nm

### Click Chemistry Handles
- Pra (Propargylglycine) — CuAAC alkyne
- Azidohomoalanine (Aha) — CuAAC azide

### Fluorescent Probes
- 7-Aza-Trp, ANAP (coumarin-based)
